\section{Overview}

This chapter presents the experimental results from the comprehensive factorial study of communication range effects on multi-agent fruit harvesting. We begin with descriptive statistics and visualizations, followed by inferential statistical analyses including ANOVA, mixed-effects models, and post-hoc comparisons. Results are organized by research question, with separate analyses for Static and Replenishing resource dynamics.
